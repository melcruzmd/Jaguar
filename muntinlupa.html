How to Use This App

Here's a quick guide to what this app can do and how to use it.

🏠 Browse Health Topics

From the Home screen, tap any card to read guidance on that topic. Tap the logo at the top anytime to return Home.

🔍 Search

Tap the search bar at the top and type a symptom or topic to jump straight to the relevant section.

📋 My Logs

Keep your own blood pressure and blood sugar readings, with date and time. Tap "View all" to see your full history.

💊 My Medicine Tracker

Use the personal checklist to record medicines you already take and monitor your intake using a routine familiar to you.

⚖️ BMI & Weight

Calculate your BMI and track your weight over time with a simple graph.

🌐 Switch Language

Tap EN or FIL at the top right anytime to switch the app's language.

🔒 Your Privacy

Your records are stored only in this browser on this device and are not intentionally sent to BIDA Health. Anyone who can open this app on your device may be able to view them.

💾 Back Up Your Data

On the My Logs page, tap "Download Backup" to save a copy of everything as one file.
